StudyPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Summary

StudyPilot is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. The developer does not operate any server and does not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

Data stored locally

Courses, schedules, notes, study records and the files you add are stored in a local database and folder on your computer. You can delete them at any time by removing the application data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

  • If you connect Google Drive, StudyPilot uploads the course files you add to a StudyPilot folder in your own Google Drive.
  • It uses only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which lets the app access only files it created. It cannot see or modify any other file in your Drive.
  • It also reads your Google account email address (via the Drive "about" endpoint) only to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on your computer, in the Windows Credential Manager. They are never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • When you delete a file in StudyPilot, its Drive copy is moved to your Drive trash (not permanently deleted).
  • You can disconnect at any time from StudyPilot Settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

StudyPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.

AI features (optional)

If you configure an AI provider in Settings (a local model such as Ollama, or a cloud API with your own key), the text you send in AI features is sent directly from your computer to that provider. Google Drive data is not sent to AI providers.
